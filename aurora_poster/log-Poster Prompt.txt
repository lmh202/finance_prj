system architecture/framework  (including our engines) 
```text
 INPUT SIGNALS -- recomputed fresh every time a holding is evaluated
 --------------------------------------------------------------------------
 [ STRATEGY ]         [ NEWS ]            [ PORTFOLIO HEALTH ]  [ RISK LEVEL ]
 Trend read on this   Today's headline    Is the whole          How risky is
 holding: Buy / Add /  tone on this         portfolio in good     this holding
 Trim / Sell / Hold    holding, incl. an    shape right now:      right now: a
                       urgent-event         a 0-100 score         0-100 percentile
                       override
       |                    |                    |                    |
       +--------------------+---------+----------+--------------------+
                                       |
                                       v
 --------------------------------------------------------------------------
 FUSION -- one engine, four ordered steps, nothing ever averaged together
 --------------------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 1   Sets the initial direction: Buy, Add, Trim, Sell, or Hold, plus a starting CONFIDENCE.
  STEP 2   News moves CONFIDENCE only -- and can flip direction ONLY through a rare, triple-gated "critical event" override (loud enough, relevant enough, and important enough).
  STEP 3   Portfolio Health nudges CONFIDENCE only. It never touches direction.
  STEP 4   Risk / Volatility scales POSITION SIZE only. It never touches direction or confidence.

  Every step is logged in a visible ledger, so the final decision is always explainable and never a black box.


                                       |
                                       v
 --------------------------------------------------------------------------
 RECOMMENDATION -- one decision per holding
 --------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Action       New Buy / Add / Trim / Close / Hold
  Confidence   0-100%, built from the ledger above
  Risk band    Low / Moderate / Elevated / High -- with every
               contributing driver named, not folded into one number
```
